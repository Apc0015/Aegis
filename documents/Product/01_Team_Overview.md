# Product Team Overview

## Mission
Define and prioritize what we build to deliver maximum customer value.

## Team Structure (6 members)

### Leadership
- VP Product (1)
- Director of Product (1)

### Product Managers
- Senior Product Manager (2)
- Product Manager (2)

## Areas of Responsibility

### B2B Product Line
Led by Senior PM focusing on enterprise features and integrations.

### Consumer Product Line  
Led by Senior PM focusing on user experience and growth.

### Platform & Infrastructure
Product Manager supporting internal tools and developer experience.

## Product Process

### Quarterly Planning
- OKR definition with leadership
- Resource allocation
- Major initiative prioritization

### Sprint Involvement
PMs attend engineering standups and planning meetings.

### Release Management
- Feature flag coordination
- Launch sequencing
- Communication planning

## Key Metrics
- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- Feature adoption rates
- Customer satisfaction (NPS)
- Revenue impact of features

## Locations
- New York: 50%
- San Francisco: 33%
- Remote: 17%
