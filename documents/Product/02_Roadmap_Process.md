# Product Roadmap Process

## Roadmap Structure

### Now (Current Quarter)
Committed work in progress. Teams actively building.

### Next (Following Quarter)
High-confidence planned work. Requirements being defined.

### Later (Beyond 2 Quarters)
Strategic initiatives. Subject to change based on learning.

## Input Sources

### Customer Feedback
- Support tickets analysis
- Customer interviews
- NPS feedback
- Usage analytics

### Business Needs
- Revenue opportunities
- Competitive positioning
- Partnership requirements
- Regulatory changes

### Technical Input
- Engineering architecture recommendations
- Technical debt considerations
- Infrastructure scaling needs

## Prioritization Framework

### RICE Scoring
- **R**each: How many users affected?
- **I**mpact: How much value per user?
- **C**onfidence: How sure are we?
- **E**ffort: Person-months required?

Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

### Strategic Buckets
1. Core product improvements (40%)
2. Growth initiatives (30%)
3. Technical foundation (20%)
4. Experiments & bets (10%)

## Review Cadence
- Weekly: Feature prioritization
- Monthly: Roadmap health check
- Quarterly: Full roadmap review
- Annually: Strategic planning
