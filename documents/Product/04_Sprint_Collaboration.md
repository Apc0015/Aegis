# Product-Engineering Collaboration

## Sprint Participation

### Sprint Planning
Product attends and provides:
- User stories with acceptance criteria
- Context on business priority
- Answers to "why" questions

### Daily Standups
Optional attendance for Product:
- Listen for blockers
- Clarify requirements as needed
- Avoid disrupting engineering flow

### Sprint Review
Product leads:
- Demo coordination
- Stakeholder communication
- Feedback collection

### Retrospectives
Product participates as team member:
- Process improvements
- Communication feedback
- Planning accuracy review

## Requirements Documentation

### User Stories
```
As a [type of user],
I want [goal],
So that [benefit].

Acceptance Criteria:
1. [Criteria 1]
2. [Criteria 2]
3. [Criteria 3]
```

### Technical Design Review
Product attends TDD reviews for:
- Alignment with user needs
- Scope clarification
- Trade-off discussions

## Communication Channels

### Slack
- #product-updates: Announcements
- #product-questions: Q&A
- #feature-[name]: Specific features

### Documentation
- Confluence: PRDs, specs
- Figma: Designs and prototypes
- Jira: Tickets and sprint board

### Meetings
- Weekly product-engineering sync
- Monthly roadmap alignment
- Quarterly planning

## Escalation Path
1. PM & Engineering Manager
2. Director of Product & VP Engineering
3. VP Product & CTO
