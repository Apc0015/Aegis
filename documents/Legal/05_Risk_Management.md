# Risk Management

## Risk Management Framework

### Philosophy
Proactively identify, assess, and mitigate risks to protect company value and enable confident decision-making.

### Risk Categories
1. **Strategic**: Market, competition, business model
2. **Operational**: Process, people, systems
3. **Financial**: Cash flow, pricing, credit
4. **Legal**: Contracts, compliance, litigation
5. **Reputational**: Brand, public perception
6. **Technology**: Security, availability, scalability

### Risk Appetite
- **Low**: Legal compliance, data security
- **Medium**: Vendor relationships, employment
- **Higher**: Product innovation, market expansion

## Risk Assessment Process

### Identification
- Annual risk assessment survey
- Department risk reviews
- Incident analysis
- External threat monitoring

### Assessment Criteria
| Factor | Scale |
|--------|-------|
| Likelihood | Rare (1) to Almost Certain (5) |
| Impact | Insignificant (1) to Catastrophic (5) |
| **Risk Score** | **Likelihood × Impact** |

### Risk Rating
| Score | Rating | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 20-25 | Critical | Immediate mitigation |
| 12-16 | High | Priority mitigation |
| 6-9 | Medium | Planned mitigation |
| 1-4 | Low | Monitor |

2. Document preservation

4. Legal counsel coordination

## Litigation Management

### Litigation Philosophy
- Avoid litigation where possible
- Aggressive defense when necessary
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Business outcome focus

### Litigation Types
- Employment disputes
- Contract disputes
- IP infringement
- Regulatory actions
- Consumer claims

### Outside Counsel Management
- Panel of approved firms
- Budget requirements
- Monthly reporting
- Quarterly business reviews
- Alternative fee arrangements

### Litigation Holds
When litigation reasonably anticipated:
- Preserve relevant documents
- Suspend deletion policies
- Notify custodians
- Document compliance

## Crisis Management

### Crisis Scenarios
- Data breach
- Executive misconduct
- Major litigation
- Regulatory investigation
- Product incident
- PR crisis

### Response Team
- CEO
- General Counsel
- CFO
- VP Marketing/Communications
- CTO (if technical)

### Response Process
1. **Immediate** (0-4 hours)
   - Assemble crisis team
   - Assess situation
   - Initial containment
   - Preserve evidence

2. **Short-term** (4-24 hours)
   - Stakeholder notifications
   - Legal strategy
   - Communication plan
   - Resource mobilization

3. **Medium-term** (1-7 days)
   - Investigation
   - Remediation
   - External communications
   - Regulatory engagement

4. **Long-term** (ongoing)
   - Resolution
   - Learning
   - Policy updates
   - Insurance claims
