# Legal Team Overview

## Mission
Protect the company's interests while enabling business growth through sound legal counsel and risk management.

## Team Structure (2 members)

### Leadership
- General Counsel (1)

### Legal Support
- Legal Counsel / Paralegal (1)

## Functional Areas

### Corporate Governance
- Board meeting support
- Corporate records maintenance
- Equity plan administration
- Subsidiary management

### Commercial Contracts
- Customer contract negotiation
- Vendor agreement review
- Partnership agreements
- Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)

### Employment Law
- Employee handbook updates
- Policy compliance review
- Employment agreements
- Termination support

### Intellectual Property
- Trademark portfolio management
- IP licensing agreements
- Patent coordination (external counsel)
- Copyright protection

### Privacy and Compliance
- Privacy policy maintenance
- GDPR/CCPA compliance
- Data processing agreements
- Security incident response

## Key Responsibilities

### Contract Management
- Template creation and maintenance
- Negotiation support
- Approval workflow management
- Contract repository maintenance

### Risk Management
- Risk assessment
- Insurance coordination
- Litigation management (external)
- Regulatory compliance

### Corporate Development
- Due diligence support
- Transaction documentation
- Integration legal matters
- SEC filings (when applicable)

## Engagement Model

### Self-Service
- Standard templates available
- Contract playbooks
- FAQ resources
- Training materials

### Legal Review Required
- Non-standard terms
- High-value deals (threshold: $100K+)
- Complex agreements
- Strategic partnerships

## Key Metrics
- Contract turnaround time: <3 days
- Legal spend vs budget
- Outstanding litigation
- Compliance audit results
