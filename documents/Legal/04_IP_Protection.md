# Intellectual Property Protection

## IP Strategy Overview

### Objectives
- Protect company innovations
- Avoid infringement of third-party rights
- Create competitive barriers
- Build company value

### IP Types
- **Patents**: Inventions and processes
- **Trademarks**: Brand names and logos
- **Copyrights**: Original works (code, content)
- **Trade Secrets**: Confidential business information

### Trademark Guidelines
**Proper Use**
- Use as adjective, not noun
- Use trademark symbol (™ or ®)
- Maintain consistent branding
- Monitor for infringement

**Do Not**
- Modify trademark
- Use in possessive form
- Use as verb
- Allow genericide

### Trademark Clearance
- Search before adopting new marks
- Legal review of proposed marks
- Registration in key markets
- Monitoring services

## Trade Secrets

### Definition
Information that:
- Has economic value from secrecy
- Is subject to reasonable efforts to maintain secrecy
- Is not generally known

### Examples
- Customer lists
- Pricing strategies
- Product roadmaps
- Algorithms
- Business processes

### Protection Measures
- Confidentiality agreements
- Access controls
- Document labeling
- Exit procedures
- Training

### Trade Secret Policy
- Label confidential documents
- Limit access to need-to-know
- Secure physical and digital assets
- Report suspected theft
