# Compliance Framework

## Compliance Program Overview

### Purpose
Ensure adherence to laws, regulations, and company policies while maintaining ethical business practices.

### Scope
- Regulatory compliance
- Data privacy
- Security standards
- Industry certifications
- Internal policies

### Governance
- General Counsel: Overall responsibility
- Compliance Committee: Cross-functional oversight
- Department heads: Functional accountability
- All employees: Individual compliance

## Data Privacy Compliance

### GDPR (EU Residents)
**Key Requirements**
- Lawful basis for processing
- Data subject rights (access, deletion, portability)
- Privacy by design
- Data breach notification (72 hours)
- DPO requirement (if applicable)

**Our Compliance**
- Privacy policy updated
- DPA in place with vendors
- Data processing records
- Subject rights request process
- Security measures

### CCPA/CPRA (California Residents)
**Key Requirements**
- Consumer rights (know, delete, opt-out)
- Privacy notice
- Service provider contracts
- Do Not Sell (if applicable)

**Our Compliance**
- California privacy policy
- Consumer request portal
- Vendor assessments
- Training for customer-facing roles

### Other Privacy Laws
- Virginia CDPA
- Colorado CPA
- Connecticut CTDPA
- Utah UCPA

## Incident Management

### Incident Classification
- **Critical**: Data breach, security incident
- **High**: Regulatory violation
- **Medium**: Policy violation
- **Low**: Documentation gap

### Response Process
1. Detection and reporting
2. Assessment and containment
3. Investigation
4. Remediation
5. Notification (if required)
6. Documentation
7. Lessons learned

### Breach Notification Requirements
| Regulation | Timeline | Who to Notify |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| GDPR | 72 hours | Supervisory authority |
| State breach laws | Varies | Affected individuals |
| Customer contracts | Per contract | Affected customers |

## Reporting and Escalation

### Compliance Reporting
- Quarterly compliance report
- Board compliance updates (annual)
- Incident reports
- Audit findings

### Escalation Path
1. Employee → Manager
2. Manager → Legal/Compliance
3. Legal → Compliance Committee
4. Committee → Board (if material)

## Questions and Concerns

### Reporting Violations
- Email: compliance@company.com
- Anonymous: Ethics hotline (if available)
- Manager or HR
- Legal department

### No Retaliation
Company prohibits retaliation against employees who report violations in good faith.
