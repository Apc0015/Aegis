## Vendor Selection Process

### Step 1: Requirements Definition
- Document business needs
- Define evaluation criteria
- Establish budget
- Identify stakeholders

### Step 2: Vendor Identification
- Existing vendor evaluation
- Market research
- Peer recommendations
- RFP issuance (for >$50K)

### Step 3: Evaluation
| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Cost | 30% | Total cost of ownership |
| Capability | 25% | Technical fit |
| Reliability | 20% | Track record |
| Service | 15% | Support quality |
| Security | 10% | Data protection |

### Step 4: Selection and Negotiation
- Reference checks
- Contract negotiation
- Legal review
- Security assessment

### Step 5: Onboarding
- Contract execution
- System setup
- Training
- SLA establishment

## Contract Management

### Approval Levels
| Contract Value | Approvers |
|----------------|-----------|
| <$10K | Department head |
| $10K - $50K | Department head + Finance |
| $50K - $100K | VP + Finance + Legal |
| >$100K | CFO + Legal + CEO |

### Key Terms
- Payment terms: Net 30 standard
- Auto-renewal: Must be explicit
- Termination: 30-90 day notice
- Liability: Mutual limitation

### Documentation
- Master vendor file
- Executed contracts
- Insurance certificates
- Security assessments
