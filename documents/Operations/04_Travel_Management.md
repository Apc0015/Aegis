# Travel Management

## Travel Policy Summary

### Booking
- Use corporate travel portal (Concur)
- Book 14+ days in advance when possible
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Pre-approval required for international travel

### Approval Requirements
| Travel Type | Approver |
|-------------|----------|
| Domestic | Manager |
| International | Manager + VP |
| Conference | Manager + Department budget |
| Customer visit | Manager |

## Booking Process

### Step 1: Request Approval
- Submit travel request in Concur
- Include business justification
- Estimate costs
- Get manager approval

### Step 2: Book Travel
- Search and compare options
- Select flights within policy
- Book hotels within limits
- Add rental car if needed

### Step 3: Confirmation
- Itinerary sent to email
- Calendar invites created
- Trip added to Concur profile
- Mobile app updated

### Step 4: Expense
- Trip automatically creates expense report
- Add receipts via mobile app
- Submit within 30 days

## Preferred Vendors

### Airlines
- Delta (primary domestic)
- United (secondary)
- American (for specific routes)
- International: Various based on destination

### Hotels
- Marriott portfolio (Courtyard, Renaissance)
- Hilton portfolio (Hampton, Hilton Garden Inn)
- Hyatt (where available)
- Boutique options with approval

### Car Rental
- Enterprise (primary)
- Hertz (secondary)
- Use compact/mid-size unless group travel

## Travel Support

### Emergency Support
- 24/7 assistance: 1-800-TRAVEL-1
- Lost passport procedures
- Medical emergencies
- Flight cancellations

### Travel Insurance
- Provided for all business travel
- Coverage: Medical, evacuation, cancellation
- Policy number: TRV-2026-001

### Mobile App
- Concur mobile for booking
- Real-time flight updates
- Digital receipts
- Offline access to itineraries

## International Travel

### Documentation
- Valid passport (6+ months)
- Visa requirements checked
- Travel authorization form
- Emergency contact information

### Health
- Vaccination requirements
- Travel health insurance
- Prescription medications
- Medical information card

### Security
- Travel advisory check
- Registration with embassy
- Emergency contacts
- Security briefing (high-risk areas)

## Travel Expenses

### Per Diem Rates
| Category | Domestic | International |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Meals | $75/day | $100/day |
| Incidentals | $15/day | $25/day |
| Ground transport | Actual | Actual |

### Reimbursable
- Flights and hotels
- Ground transportation
- Meals (per diem)
- Business expenses
- Communication (international plan)

### Non-Reimbursable
- Personal expenses
- Mini-bar charges
- Spouse/family expenses
- Personal entertainment
- Traffic fines

## Sustainability

### Preferred Options
- Direct flights (reduce emissions)
- Hotels with green certifications
- Public transit where practical
- Video conferencing as alternative

### Carbon Offsets
- Company offsets business travel
- Employees can opt-in for personal
- Tracked in annual sustainability report

## Travel Safety

### Pre-Trip
- Share itinerary with manager
- Emergency contacts updated
- Travel insurance verified
- Safety brief (if needed)

### During Trip
- Check in with manager (international)
- Follow local safety guidelines
- Avoid risky areas
- Keep documents secure

### Post-Trip
- Submit expenses promptly
- Share learnings
- Update travel preferences
- Feedback on vendors
