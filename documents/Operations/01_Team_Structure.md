# Operations Team Structure

## Mission
Ensure seamless business operations through efficient processes, vendor management, and workplace experience.

## Team Structure (3 members)

### Leadership
- VP Operations (1)

### Operations Management
- Operations Manager (1)
- Operations Coordinator (1)

## Functional Areas

### Workplace Operations
- Office management
- Facilities maintenance
- Health and safety compliance
- Vendor relationships

### Procurement
- Purchase order processing
- Vendor negotiations
- Contract management
- Expense management support

### Administrative
- Executive support
- Meeting coordination
- Document management
- Records retention

### Business Continuity
- Emergency preparedness
- Business continuity planning
- Disaster recovery coordination
- Risk assessment

## Key Responsibilities

### Office Management
- Space planning and allocation
- Furniture and equipment
- Supplies ordering
- Mail and package handling

### Vendor Management
- Vendor selection and onboarding
- Contract negotiations
- Performance monitoring
- Annual reviews

### Events Support
- All-hands meetings
- Executive offsites
- Customer visits
- Team events

### Travel Coordination
- Corporate travel booking
- Travel policy enforcement
- Vendor negotiations (hotels, airlines)
- Expense reporting support

## Key Metrics
- Cost per square foot
- Vendor spend under management
- Purchase order cycle time
- Office utilization rate
- Employee satisfaction (facilities)
