## Workspace Allocation

### Seat Assignments
- Assigned seats based on team/department
- Desk moves require manager approval
- Hoteling desks available for visitors
- Clean desk policy required

## Health and Safety

### Emergency Procedures
- Fire exits marked and illuminated
- Evacuation drills quarterly
- First aid kits in kitchen areas
- AED device in main lobby

### Ergonomics
- Adjustable desks available
- Ergonomic assessments on request
- Monitor arms and keyboard trays
- Footrests available

### COVID-19 Protocols
- Hand sanitizer stations throughout
- Enhanced cleaning schedule
- Sick employees stay home
- Testing kits available on request

## Supplies and Equipment

### Ordering Process
1. Submit request via Slack #office-supplies
2. Operations approves and orders
3. Typical delivery: 3-5 business days
4. Pick up from supply room

### Standard Equipment
- Laptop stand
- External monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Webcam and headset

### Special Requests
- Standing desk: Manager approval
- Multiple monitors: Business justification
- Specialized equipment: Case-by-case

## Visitor Policy

### Host Responsibilities
- Register guest 24 hours in advance
- Meet guest in lobby
- Sign in at reception
- Return visitor badge

### Visitor Hours
- Monday-Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- After hours: Security escort required

### Visitor Areas
- Meeting rooms
- Cafeteria
- Lobby
- Restrooms

## Maintenance Requests

### Submitting Requests
- Slack: #facilities-requests
- Email: facilities@company.com
- Urgent: Call office manager

### Response Times
- **Emergency**: Immediate (safety, security)
- **Urgent**: Same day (HVAC, plumbing)
- **Standard**: 3-5 business days
- **Cosmetic**: 1-2 weeks

### Common Issues
- Temperature control: Contact facilities
- Desk/chair issues: Operations team
- IT equipment: IT help desk
- Cleaning: Facilities vendor
