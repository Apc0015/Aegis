## Types of Emergencies

### Medical Emergency
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Stay with victim until help arrives
3. Notify Office Manager
4. Document incident
5. Follow up with HR

### Fire Emergency
1. Pull fire alarm
2. Evacuate building immediately
3. Do not use elevators
4. Proceed to assembly point (south parking lot)
5. Wait for all-clear from fire department

### Security Threat
1. Call 911 if immediate danger
2. Notify security and management
3. Shelter in place or evacuate per instructions
4. Follow lockdown procedures if announced
5. Cooperate with law enforcement

### Severe Weather
1. Monitor alerts via phone/email
2. Shelter in interior rooms if tornado
3. Evacuate per instructions for hurricane
4. Work from home if unsafe to travel
5. Wait for all-clear from management

### Power Outage
1. Wait 5 minutes for backup power
2. Save work and shut down computers
3. Exit using stairs (not elevators)
4. Wait for instructions from Operations
5. Work from home if extended outage

## Evacuation Procedures

### Assembly Points
**New York HQ**
- Primary: South parking lot
- Secondary: Park across street

**San Francisco**
- Primary: Plaza outside building
- Secondary: Coffee shop at corner

### Roll Call
- Department heads account for team
- Report missing persons to Operations
- Wait for all-clear
- Do not re-enter until authorized

### Mobility Impaired
- Identify during orientation
- Assigned buddy for assistance
- Use evacuation chairs in stairwells
- Priority evacuation
