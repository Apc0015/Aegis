# Engineering Team Structure

## Overview
The Engineering department consists of 18 team members across multiple disciplines and seniority levels.

## Organizational Hierarchy

### Leadership
- VP Engineering (1)
- Principal Engineer (1)
- Staff Engineers (2)

### Teams

#### Backend Engineering
- Senior Engineers (3)
- Software Engineers (4)
- Junior Engineers (2)

#### Frontend Engineering
- Senior Engineers (2)
- Software Engineers (3)

#### DevOps & Infrastructure
- Senior DevOps Engineer (1)
- DevOps Engineer (1)

## Reporting Structure
All engineers report to their respective team leads. Team leads report to Staff Engineers who coordinate with VP Engineering on strategic decisions.

## Locations
- New York HQ: 40%
- San Francisco: 30%
- Remote: 25%
- Other offices: 5%

## Employment Distribution
- Full-time: 75% (13 employees)
- Part-time: 10% (2 employees)
- Contractors: 15% (3 contractors)

## Salary Bands (2026)
- L1 (Junior): $75,000 - $95,000
- L2 (Mid): $95,000 - $130,000
- L3 (Senior): $130,000 - $170,000
- L4 (Staff): $170,000 - $200,000
- L5 (Principal/VP): $200,000 - $250,000
