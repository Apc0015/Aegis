# Engineering Benefits and Perks

## Professional Development

### Learning Budget
- $2,500 annual professional development budget
- Covers conferences, courses, certifications, books
- Pre-approval required for expenses over $500

### Conference Attendance
- Engineers encouraged to attend 1-2 conferences per year
- Popular choices: AWS re:Invent, KubeCon, PyCon, React Conf
- Company covers registration, travel, accommodation

### Certification Support
- AWS certifications fully reimbursed
- Kubernetes (CKA/CKAD) certifications covered
- Study materials and exam fees included

## Work Environment

### Equipment
- MacBook Pro or equivalent Linux laptop
- External monitor (27" 4K)
- Ergonomic chair and standing desk
- Noise-canceling headphones

### Remote Work
- Flexible hybrid schedule (3 days office, 2 remote)
- Home office stipend: $1,000 one-time
- Monthly internet reimbursement: $75

### Wellness
- $500 annual wellness stipend
- Gym membership discounts
- Mental health support via Modern Health

## Team Activities
- Monthly team lunches
- Quarterly offsite events
- Annual engineering retreat
- Hackathons twice per year

## Additional Perks
- Flexible PTO (unlimited for senior roles)
- Parental leave: 16 weeks primary, 6 weeks secondary
- 401(k) match: 100% up to 4%
- Health, dental, vision insurance
