# Engineering Performance Expectations

## Seniority Levels

### Junior Engineer (L1)
- Completes assigned tasks with guidance
- Learns codebase and development practices
- Participates in code reviews as observer
- Timeline: 0-2 years experience

### Software Engineer (L2)
- Independently completes features
- Mentors junior engineers
- Contributes to technical design discussions
- Timeline: 2-4 years experience

### Senior Engineer (L3)
- Leads medium to large projects
- Makes architectural decisions
- Cross-team collaboration
- Mentors L1 and L2 engineers
- Timeline: 4-7 years experience

### Staff Engineer (L4)
- Sets technical direction
- Resolves complex technical challenges
- Influences engineering-wide decisions
- Mentors senior engineers

### Principal Engineer / VP (L5)
- Defines long-term technical strategy
- Partners with product and business
- Industry thought leadership
- Organizational scaling

## Review Process
- Quarterly check-ins with manager
- Annual 360-degree review
- Peer feedback collected each quarter
- Self-assessment required annually

## Promotion Criteria
- Consistent performance at next level for 6+ months
- Business need for the role
- Manager and Staff Engineer endorsement
