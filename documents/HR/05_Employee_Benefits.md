# Employee Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance

### Medical Plans
**PPO Plan**
- Monthly premium: $200 (employee), $500 (family)
- Deductible: $1,000 individual / $2,000 family
- Co-pay: $25 primary, $40 specialist
- Out-of-pocket max: $5,000 / $10,000

**HMO Plan**
- Monthly premium: $150 (employee), $400 (family)
- Deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Co-pay: $15 primary, $30 specialist
- Requires primary care referral

**HDHP with HSA**
- Monthly premium: $100 (employee), $300 (family)
- Deductible: $3,000 / $6,000
- Company HSA contribution: $500/year
- Out-of-pocket max: $6,000 / $12,000

### Dental Insurance
- Monthly premium: $25 (employee), $75 (family)
- Preventive: 100% covered
- Basic procedures: 80% covered
- Major procedures: 50% covered

### Vision Insurance
- Monthly premium: $10 (employee), $25 (family)
- Annual eye exam: $10 co-pay
- Frames allowance: $150
- Contact lens allowance: $150

## Retirement Benefits

### 401(k) Plan
- Employer match: 100% of first 4%
- Immediate vesting
- Roth option available
- Loan provision available

### Example
Employee contributes 4% of $100,000 salary = $4,000
Company match = $4,000
Total contribution = $8,000
