# HR Team Structure

## Mission
Attract, develop, and retain exceptional talent while fostering a positive workplace culture.

## Team Structure (3 members)

### Leadership
- VP People (Chief People Officer) (1)

### HR Operations
- HR Manager (1)
- HR Specialist (1)

## Functional Areas

### Talent Acquisition
- Job posting and sourcing
- Interview coordination
- Candidate experience
- Offer negotiation

### Employee Relations
- Onboarding and offboarding
- Performance management support
- Conflict resolution
- Employee engagement

### Compensation and Benefits
- Salary benchmarking
- Benefits administration
- Open enrollment
- Equity management

### HR Operations
- Policy development
- Compliance and reporting
- HRIS management
- Records keeping

## Key Responsibilities

### Recruiting
- Manage full-cycle recruiting process
- Build talent pipeline
- Employer branding
- Interview training for managers

### Employee Experience
- Design onboarding program
- Coordinate training and development
- Manage employee recognition programs
- Conduct exit interviews

### Compliance
- Maintain employee handbook
- Ensure EEOC compliance
- Handle workplace investigations
- Manage worker's compensation

## Key Metrics
- Time to fill: < 45 days
- Offer acceptance rate: > 80%
- Employee retention: > 90%
- Engagement score: > 4.0/5.0

## HRIS
System: BambooHR
- Employee database
- Time off tracking
- Benefits enrollment
- Reporting and analytics
