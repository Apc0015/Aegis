# Recruiting Process

## Requisition Approval

### Step 1: Hiring Manager Request
- Complete headcount request form
- Define role requirements
- Set salary range
- Get budget approval

### Step 2: Job Description Creation
- Use templates as starting point
- Include required vs preferred qualifications
- Define success metrics for role
- Get approval from HR and hiring manager

### Step 3: Job Posting
- Post to company careers page
- Distribute to job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
- Share with employee referral network
- Source passive candidates

## Interview Process

### Phone Screen (30 min)
- Conducted by HR
- Confirm basic qualifications
- Assess culture fit
- Share salary expectations
- Schedule next steps

### Technical/Skills Assessment
- Varies by role
- May include case study, coding exercise, or presentation
- Evaluate job-specific competencies

### Team Interviews (2-3 rounds)
- Meet potential teammates
- Assess collaboration skills
- Deep dive on experience
- Answer candidate questions

### Final Interview
- Senior leader conversation
- Culture and values alignment
- Role and expectations review
- Offer preparation

## Evaluation Criteria

### Technical Skills (40%)
- Required qualifications
- Past performance
- Problem-solving ability

### Culture Fit (30%)
- Alignment with values
- Communication style
- Collaboration approach

### Growth Potential (20%)
- Learning agility
- Career trajectory
- Adaptability

### Diversity (10%)
- Unique perspectives
- Background and experience
- Potential contributions

## Offer Process

### Approval Chain
1. Hiring manager recommendation
2. HR review (compensation, equity)
3. Finance approval (budget)
4. VP sign-off (if VP+ level)

### Offer Components
- Base salary
- Equity/options
- Benefits overview
- Start date
- Sign-on bonus (if applicable)

### Timeline
- Offer extended within 48 hours of final interview
- Candidate given 5 business days to respond
- Weekly follow-up during decision period
