# Performance Review Process

## Review Cadence

### Quarterly Check-ins
- Informal conversations
- Goal progress review
- Feedback exchange
- Development planning
- No formal rating

### Annual 360 Review
- Self-assessment
- Peer feedback (3-5 colleagues)
- Manager review
- Upward feedback (for managers)
- Formal performance rating

## Rating Scale

### Exceeds Expectations (5)
- Consistently exceeds goals
- Demonstrates exceptional performance
- Ready for next level

### Strong Performance (4)
- Exceeds most goals
- Solid contributor
- Exemplifies company values

### Meets Expectations (3)
- Achieves defined goals
- Reliable performer
- On track for role

### Needs Improvement (2)
- Misses some goals
- Development plan required
- Check-ins every 2 weeks

### Unsatisfactory (1)
- Fails to meet expectations
- Performance plan required
- Improvement timeline defined

## Self-Assessment

### Questions to Address
1. What were your key accomplishments this year?
2. What goals did you not achieve and why?
3. What skills did you develop?
4. What support do you need going forward?
5. What are your career aspirations?

## Peer Feedback

### Process
- Employee nominates 3-5 peers
- Manager approves list
- Peers complete questionnaire (15 min)
- Feedback aggregated and anonymized

### Questions
- What does this person do well?
- What could they improve?
- How do they embody company values?
- Would you want to work with them again?

## Manager Review

### Components
- Goal achievement assessment
- Competency evaluation
- Values alignment
- Development opportunities
- Promotion readiness

### Calibration
- Managers meet to align on ratings
- Ensure consistency across teams
- Identify high and low performers
- Discuss edge cases

## Development Planning

### Individual Development Plan (IDP)
- Strengths to leverage
- Areas for improvement
- Learning opportunities
- Stretch assignments
- Timeline and milestones

### Career Pathing
- Current level competencies
- Next level requirements
- Gap analysis
- Support needed

## Review Timeline

### November
- Process communication
- Self-assessments due
- Peer feedback requests sent

### December
- Peer feedback collection
- Manager reviews written
- Calibration meetings

### January
- Review meetings scheduled
- Performance discussions
- Compensation decisions
- Promotions announced

### February
- IDPs finalized
- Goal setting for new year
- Development plans launched
