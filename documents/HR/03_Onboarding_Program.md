# New Employee Onboarding Program

## Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

### 1 Week Before Start
- Send welcome email with schedule
- Ship equipment (laptop, monitor, accessories)
- Create accounts and access provisioning
- Assign onboarding buddy

### Day Before Start
- Confirm first day logistics
- Prepare workspace
- Send team introduction email
- Schedule welcome lunch

## First Day

### Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
- 9:00 - Welcome and office tour
- 9:30 - IT setup and account verification
- 10:30 - HR paperwork and benefits enrollment
- 11:30 - Meet with direct manager

### Afternoon (1:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
- 1:00 - Team lunch
- 2:30 - Department overview
- 3:30 - Product demo
- 4:30 - End of day check-in with buddy

## First Week

### Day 2-3: Foundations
- Complete required training modules
- Review company handbook and policies
- Set up development environment
- Schedule 1:1s with key stakeholders

### Day 4-5: Immersion
- Shadow team meetings
- Review current projects
- Complete first small task
- Participate in team activity

## First 30 Days

### Week 2-3: Learning
- Complete role-specific training
- Deep dive into product and customers
- Build relationships across teams
- Attend relevant meetings

### Week 4: Contributing
- Take ownership of first project
- Provide input in team discussions
- Complete first deliverable
- Check-in with manager on progress

## 90-Day Plan

### Days 1-30: Learn
- Understand the business
- Meet the team
- Master tools and processes

### Days 31-60: Contribute
- Deliver first project
- Identify quick wins
- Build cross-functional relationships

### Days 61-90: Lead
- Take initiative on improvements
- Mentor newer team members
- Present learnings to team

## Onboarding Checklist

### HR Tasks
- [ ] I-9 verification completed
- [ ] Benefits enrollment
- [ ] Emergency contacts collected
- [ ] Payroll setup
- [ ] Handbook acknowledgment

### IT Tasks
- [ ] Laptop configured
- [ ] Email and Slack access
- [ ] VPN setup
- [ ] Security training
- [ ] Password manager setup

### Manager Tasks
- [ ] Role expectations document shared
- [ ] 30-60-90 day plan created
- [ ] Key contacts introduced
- [ ] First project assigned
- [ ] Weekly check-ins scheduled

## Buddy Program

### Buddy Responsibilities
- Answer day-to-day questions
- Share unwritten rules and norms
- Introduce to other team members
- Check in weekly for first month

### Buddy Selection
- Same department, different team
- Tenure of 1+ years
- Volunteer basis
- Recognized in performance review
