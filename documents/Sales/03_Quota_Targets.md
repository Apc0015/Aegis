## Quota Planning Cycle

### Annual
- Baseline on previous year performance
- Account for product/market changes
- Territory rebalancing
- Capacity planning

### Quarterly
- Adjust for seasonality
- Account for pipeline health
- Individual performance review
- Stretch goals defined

## Quota Relief
Quota relief granted for:
- Approved PTO > 5 days
- Medical leave
- Territory changes
- Product delays

## Accelerators
Above 100% quota attainment:
- 100-110%: 1.2x commission rate
- 110-125%: 1.5x commission rate
- 125%+: 2x commission rate

## President's Club
Top 20% of performers qualify:
- Annual sales retreat
- $5K bonus
- Stock option grant
- Public recognition
