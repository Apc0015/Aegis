# Sales Process and Methodology

## Sales Methodology
We use MEDDIC for enterprise deals and Solution Selling for mid-market.

### MEDDIC Framework
- **M**etrics: Quantifiable business impact
- **E**conomic Buyer: Decision maker with budget
- **D**ecision Criteria: Requirements evaluation
- **D**ecision Process: Steps and timeline
- **I**dentify Pain: Business challenges
- **C**hampion: Internal advocate

## Sales Process

### Stage 1: Prospecting (SDR)
- Target account research
- Personalized outreach
- LinkedIn engagement
- Cold calling (50 calls/day target)

### Stage 2: Discovery (AE)
- Needs assessment
- Stakeholder mapping
- Pain point identification
- Budget confirmation

### Stage 3: Demo
- Tailored to use case
- Technical validation
- ROI calculator
- Competitive differentiation

### Stage 4: Proposal
- Custom solution design
- Pricing options
- Implementation timeline
- Success metrics

### Stage 5: Negotiation
- Legal review
- Security questionnaire
- Procurement process
- Executive sponsor engagement

### Stage 6: Close
- Contract signature
- Handoff to Customer Success
- Internal celebration
- Win/loss analysis

## CRM Requirements
- All opportunities logged in Salesforce
- Weekly pipeline reviews
- Contact roles documented
- Next steps always defined
