# Sales Team Structure

## Mission
Drive revenue growth through new customer acquisition and existing account expansion.

## Team Structure (8 members)

### Leadership
- Sales Director (1)
- Sales Manager (1)

### Account Executives
- Senior Account Executive (2)
- Account Executive (3)

### Business Development
- Sales Development Rep (SDR) (1)

## Territory Distribution

### Enterprise Accounts
Led by Senior AEs:
- Fortune 500 companies
- Deals > $100K ACV
- 6-12 month sales cycles

### Mid-Market
Led by AEs:
- 100-1000 employee companies
- Deals $20K-$100K ACV
- 3-6 month sales cycles

### Outbound Prospecting
SDR handles:
- Cold outreach
- Qualification calls
- Meeting booking for AEs

## Compensation Structure

### Base + Commission
- SDR: $60K base + $15K OTE
- AE: $85K base + $85K OTE
- Senior AE: $100K base + $120K OTE
- Manager+: $130K base + $100K OTE

### Quota Attainment
- 100% = Full commission
- 80-99% = 80% commission
- 60-79% = 50% commission
- < 60% = No commission

## Sales Stages
1. Prospecting
2. Discovery
3. Demo
4. Proposal
5. Negotiation
6. Closed Won/Lost

## Key Metrics
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Annual contract value (ACV)
- Win rate
- Average sales cycle length
- Pipeline coverage (3x quota)
