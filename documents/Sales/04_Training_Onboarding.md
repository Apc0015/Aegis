# Sales Training and Onboarding

## New Hire Onboarding (4 weeks)

### Week 1: Foundation
- Company overview and culture
- Product deep dive
- Market and competition
- Tools training (CRM, outreach)

### Week 2: Process
- Sales methodology training
- Call shadowing (10+ calls)
- Email template review
- Role-playing exercises

### Week 3: Practice
- Live calling with supervision
- Demo delivery practice
- Objection handling drills
- Territory/account assignment

### Week 4: Launch
- Independent prospecting
- Manager check-ins daily
- First pipeline generation
- 30-day goal setting

## Ongoing Training

### Weekly
- Sales team standup
- Win/loss review
- Call recording review (Gong)

### Monthly
- Product update training
- Competitive intelligence
- Skills workshop

### Quarterly
- Sales kickoff (SKO)
- Strategic planning
- Team building

## Certification Requirements

### SDR Certification
- 50+ calls completed
- 10+ meetings booked
- Manager sign-off

### AE Certification
- 3 full sales cycles observed
- Solo demo delivered
- Proposal created
- Manager sign-off

## Mentorship Program
- New hires paired with experienced rep
- Weekly 1:1 check-ins
- Shadow days scheduled
- Reverse mentoring encouraged
