# Sales Compensation Plan

## Base Salary Ranges

### SDR (Sales Development Rep)
- Base: $60,000
- OTE: $75,000
- Structure: 80% base / 20% variable

### Account Executive
- Base: $85,000
- OTE: $170,000
- Structure: 50% base / 50% variable

### Senior Account Executive
- Base: $100,000
- OTE: $220,000
- Structure: 45% base / 55% variable

### Sales Manager
- Base: $130,000
- OTE: $230,000
- Structure: 55% base / 45% variable

## Commission Structure

### New Business Commission
- 10% of first year ACV
- Paid monthly upon invoice payment
- Clawback if churn within 6 months

### Expansion Commission
- 5% of expansion ARR
- Qualifies for renewals with upsell

### Multi-Year Deals
- Year 1: Full commission
- Year 2+: 50% commission

## Benefits

### Health and Wellness
- Medical, dental, vision coverage
- Mental health support
- Gym membership discount

### Time Off
- Unlimited PTO (with manager approval)
- 12 weeks parental leave
- Sick days: unlimited

### Financial
- 401(k) with 4% match
- Stock options for top performers
- Commuter benefits
- Cell phone reimbursement ($100/month)

### Perks
- Flexible work arrangements
- Home office stipend
- Professional development budget
- President's Club trip
