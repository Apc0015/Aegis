## Equipment Lifecycle

### Standard Lifecycle
| Equipment | Lifecycle | Refresh Trigger |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Laptop | 3 years | Age, damage, role change |
| Monitor | 5 years | Failure, upgrade |
| Peripherals | As needed | Failure, loss |
| Mobile | 2 years | Age, damage |

### Early Refresh
Approved for:
- Role change requiring different specs
- Equipment damage beyond repair
- Performance issues affecting productivity
- Security requirements (older OS)

### Disposition
- IT sanitizes all data
- Equipment returned to IT
- Resale or recycling coordinated
- Certificate of destruction for storage devices

## Procurement Process

### New Hires
1. HR submits equipment request (14 days before start)
2. IT reviews and approves
3. Equipment ordered or pulled from inventory
4. Device configured and shipped
5. Tracking shared with employee

### Replacement Requests
1. Employee submits via helpdesk
2. Manager approval required
3. IT validates need
4. New equipment ordered
5. Old equipment returned

### Emergency Replacement
- Lost/stolen: Immediate replacement
- Damage: Based on severity
- Failure: Temporary loaner if available

## Equipment Care

### Employee Responsibilities
- Use equipment primarily for business
- Report damage immediately
- Keep equipment secure
- Install approved software only
- Allow IT access for support

### Security
- Enable automatic lock
- Use cable lock in public spaces
- Never leave unattended in public
- Report loss/theft within 2 hours

### Maintenance
- Keep clean and dry
- Update software promptly
- Backup data regularly
- Return for servicing when needed
