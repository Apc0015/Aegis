# Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Template

## QBR Overview

### Purpose
- Review progress against goals
- Demonstrate value realized
- Align on priorities
- Plan for next quarter

### Frequency
- Enterprise: Quarterly
- Mid-market: Semi-annually
- Low-touch: Annual

### Attendees
- Customer executive sponsor
- Key stakeholders
- CSM
- Account Manager (if expansion opportunity)

---

## QBR Agenda Template

### 1. Opening (5 min)
- Welcome and introductions
- Agenda review
- Previous action items recap

### 2. Business Review (10 min)
- Customer business updates
- Industry trends discussion
- Goals and priorities check-in

### 3. Product Usage Review (15 min)
**Adoption Metrics**
- Active users vs. licenses
- Feature utilization
- Login frequency

**Success Metrics**
- KPIs achieved
- ROI calculation
- Before/after comparison

**Usage Trends**
- Month-over-month growth
- Department breakdown
- Power users identified

### 4. Value Realization (15 min)
**Quantified Value**
- Time saved
- Cost reduction
- Revenue impact
- Efficiency gains

**Qualitative Value**
- Process improvements
- Team satisfaction
- Customer feedback
- Competitive advantage

### 5. Success Plan Review (10 min)
- Goals from last quarter
- Progress assessment
- Completed milestones
- Outstanding items

### 6. Product Updates (10 min)
- New features released
- Features customer should adopt
- Roadmap preview
- Feedback solicitation

### 7. Next Quarter Planning (15 min)
**Goals for Next Quarter**
- Priority initiatives
- Success metrics
- Resource requirements
- Timeline

**Expansion Opportunities**
- Additional use cases
- User growth plans
- Feature needs
- Integration opportunities

### 8. Feedback and Support (5 min)
- Satisfaction check
- Support experience
- Product feedback
- Relationship health

### 9. Closing (5 min)
- Action items summary
- Next meeting scheduled
- Thank you and appreciation

---

## Preparation Checklist

### 1 Week Before
- [ ] Schedule meeting with customer
- [ ] Collect usage data
- [ ] Review previous QBR notes
- [ ] Prepare draft presentation

### 3 Days Before
- [ ] Complete presentation
- [ ] Internal review with team
- [ ] Send pre-read materials
- [ ] Confirm attendees

### Day Of
- [ ] Test technology
- [ ] Join early
- [ ] Record meeting (with permission)
- [ ] Take detailed notes

### 2 Days After
- [ ] Send recap email
- [ ] Share action items
- [ ] Update CRM
- [ ] Schedule follow-ups

## Best Practices

### Do's
- Focus on customer outcomes, not product features
- Bring data and examples
- Listen more than talk
- Leave time for discussion
- Follow up on commitments

### Don'ts
- Read slides verbatim
- Focus on problems without solutions
- Go over time
- Make promises you can't keep
- Skip preparation

### Remote QBRs
- Use video (cameras on)
- Share screen effectively
- Use interactive tools (polls)
- Build in breaks
- Send materials in advance
