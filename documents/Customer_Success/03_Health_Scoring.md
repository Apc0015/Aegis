# Customer Health Scoring

## Health Score Components

### Product Usage (40%)
**Active Users**
- Score 100: >80% of licenses used
- Score 75: 60-80% of licenses used
- Score 50: 40-60% of licenses used
- Score 25: <40% of licenses used

**Feature Adoption**
- Score 100: Using 5+ core features
- Score 75: Using 3-4 core features
- Score 50: Using 2 core features
- Score 25: Using 1 core feature

**Login Frequency**
- Score 100: Daily usage
- Score 75: Weekly usage
- Score 50: Monthly usage
- Score 25: Quarterly or less

### Engagement (30%)
**Meeting Attendance**
- Score 100: Attends all scheduled meetings
- Score 75: Attends 75% of meetings
- Score 50: Attends 50% of meetings
- Score 25: Attends <50% of meetings

**Communication Responsiveness**
- Score 100: Responds within 24 hours
- Score 75: Responds within 48 hours
- Score 50: Responds within 1 week
- Score 25: Poor responsiveness

**Content Engagement**
- Score 100: High engagement (webinars, guides)
- Score 75: Moderate engagement
- Score 50: Low engagement
- Score 25: No engagement

### Support Health (20%)
**Ticket Volume**
- Score 100: No tickets last 90 days
- Score 75: 1-2 tickets, resolved quickly
- Score 50: 3-5 tickets
- Score 25: >5 tickets or escalations

**Ticket Sentiment**
- Score 100: All positive feedback
- Score 75: Neutral feedback
- Score 50: Some frustration
- Score 25: Multiple complaints

### Business Outcomes (10%)
**Goal Achievement**
- Score 100: Exceeding success metrics
- Score 75: Meeting success metrics
- Score 50: Partial achievement
- Score 25: Not achieving goals

## Health Score Ranges

| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 90-100 | Healthy | Maintain, identify advocates |
| 70-89 | At Risk | Monitor closely, proactive outreach |
| 50-69 | Unhealthy | Intervention required, escalation |
| <50 | Critical | Executive escalation, recovery plan |

## Health Score Reviews

### Weekly
- Critical accounts review
- Red flag identification
- Action item assignment

### Monthly
- Full health score review
- Trend analysis
- Intervention planning

### Quarterly
- Health score calibration
- Scoring model adjustments
- Benchmark updates

## Risk Signals

### Product Risks
- Declining usage
- Low feature adoption
- Data export activity

### Engagement Risks
- Ghosted meetings
- Executive sponsor departure
- Champion leaving

### Business Risks
- Mergers/acquisitions
- Leadership changes
- Budget cuts
- Competitive evaluation

## Playbooks by Health Score

### Healthy (90-100)
- Quarterly check-ins
- Expansion conversations
- Reference requests
- Case study opportunities

### At Risk (70-89)
- Monthly calls
- Usage review
- Success plan refresh
- Additional training

### Unhealthy (50-69)
- Bi-weekly calls
- Executive sponsor engagement
- Detailed recovery plan
- Escalation to leadership

### Critical (<50)
- Weekly executive calls
- VP/C-level engagement
- Formal recovery plan
- Churn prevention playbook
