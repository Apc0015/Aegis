# Renewal Management Process

## Renewal Timeline

### 120 Days Before Renewal
- Account health assessment
- Product usage review
- Stakeholder mapping update
- Expansion opportunity identification

### 90 Days Before Renewal
- Renewal forecast submission
- Risk assessment completed
- Executive sponsor engagement
- Success metrics review

### 60 Days Before Renewal
- Formal renewal conversation
- Contract terms review
- Pricing discussion
- Expansion proposal (if applicable)

### 30 Days Before Renewal
- Contract sent for signature
- Procurement process support
- Legal review coordination
- Final approval obtained

### 15 Days Before Renewal
- Contract status check
- Escalation if not signed
- Contingency planning
- Finance notification

### Renewal Date
- Celebrate with customer
- Thank you communication
- Reference request
- Expansion plan for next term

## Renewal Categories

### Automatic Renewals
- Healthy customers (>90 score)
- No changes to scope
- Standard terms
- Auto-renewal clause triggered

### Managed Renewals
- Requires active management
- Scope changes expected
- Negotiation anticipated
- Multi-year options presented

### At-Risk Renewals
- Health score <70
- Competitive evaluation
- Budget pressure
- Executive intervention required

## Renewal Playbooks

### Low Touch (Digital)
- Email nurture campaign
- Self-service renewal portal
- Automated reminders
- Support as needed

### Medium Touch (CSM)
- CSM outreach 90 days prior
- Quarterly business review
- Usage report sharing
- Expansion conversation

### High Touch (CSM + Executive)
- Executive sponsor engagement
- Custom business case
- Roadmap alignment
- Strategic partnership discussion

## Expansion Strategy

### Expansion Types
1. **Seat expansion**: More users
2. **Feature expansion**: Additional modules
3. **Usage expansion**: Higher volume limits
4. **Service expansion**: Professional services

### Expansion Triggers
- Usage approaching limits
- Team growth communicated
- New use cases identified
- Feature requests indicate needs

### Expansion Conversation
- Value realization review
- ROI quantification
- Success metrics alignment
- Proposal presentation

## Churn Prevention

### Early Warning Signs
- Declining health score
- Reduced engagement
- Support ticket escalations
- Executive sponsor departure

### Intervention Strategies
- Executive business review
- Success plan refresh
- Additional resources
- Pricing flexibility
- Product roadmap alignment

### Churn Analysis
- Exit interview
- Reason categorization
- Pattern identification
- Product feedback
- Win-back campaign (if applicable)

## Renewal Metrics

### Team KPIs
- Gross retention rate: >90%
- Net retention rate: >110%
- Logo retention: >95%
- Time to renewal: <30 days before expiration

### Individual CSM KPIs
- Renewal rate: >95%
- Expansion rate: >20%
- Health score maintenance
- Customer satisfaction
