# Customer Success Team Overview

## Mission
Ensure customers achieve their desired outcomes and maximize product value.

## Team Structure (2 members)

### Leadership
- Customer Success Director (1)

### Customer Success Managers
- Senior CSM (1)

## Coverage Model
- Each CSM manages 30-40 accounts
- Segmented by customer size and complexity
- Proactive engagement model
- Digital CS for low-touch segment

## Functional Areas

### Onboarding
- Implementation project management
- Training delivery
- Value definition
- Success planning

### Adoption
- Usage monitoring
- Best practices sharing
- Feature education
- Use case expansion

### Retention
- Health score monitoring
- Risk identification
- Renewal management
- Escalation handling

### Expansion
- Upsell identification
- Cross-sell opportunities
- Reference recruitment
- Case study development

## Customer Segments

### Enterprise
- Annual contract value: $50K+
- Dedicated CSM
- Quarterly business reviews
- Custom success plans

### Mid-Market
- Annual contract value: $10K-$50K
- Shared CSM
- Monthly check-ins
- Standard playbooks

### Digital Touch
- Annual contract value: <$10K
- Automated nurture
- Webinars and resources
- Community access

## Key Metrics

### Health Metrics
- Customer health score
- Product adoption rate
- Feature utilization
- Support ticket volume

### Business Metrics
- Net revenue retention: >110%
- Gross retention: >90%
- Expansion revenue: 20% of ARR
- Logo churn: <5% annually

### Engagement Metrics
- NPS score: >50
- CSAT: >4.5/5
- Meeting completion rate: >80%
- Content engagement
