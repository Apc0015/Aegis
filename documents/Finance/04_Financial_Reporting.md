## Key Financial Metrics

### Revenue Metrics
| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | Sum of monthly subscriptions | Growth > 10% MoM |
| Annual Recurring Revenue | MRR × 12 | Growth > 100% YoY |
| Net Revenue Retention | (Start + Expansion - Churn) / Start | > 110% |
| Gross Revenue Retention | (Start - Churn) / Start | > 90% |

### Unit Economics
| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Customer Acquisition Cost | Sales & Marketing / New Customers | < $5,000 |
| Lifetime Value | ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate | > $50,000 |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | LTV / CAC | > 3x |
| Payback Period | CAC / (ARPU × GM) | < 18 months |

### Efficiency Metrics
| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | > 75% |
| Operating Margin | Operating Income / Revenue | > 20% |
| Rule of 40 | Growth % + Profit Margin % | > 40% |
| Burn Multiple | Net Burn / Net New ARR | < 1.5x |
