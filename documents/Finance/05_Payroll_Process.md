# Payroll Process

## Payroll Schedule

### Pay Dates
- **Semi-monthly**: 15th and last day of month
- **Direct deposit**: Funds available by 12:01 AM on pay date
- **Pay stubs**: Available in HRIS 2 days before pay date

### Cutoff Dates
- Time entry: 3 business days before pay date
- Expense reports: 5 business days before pay date
- New hire paperwork: 7 business days before first pay date

## Payroll Components

### Regular Pay
- Base salary / 24 pay periods
- Prorated for new hires
- Adjusted for unpaid time off

### Variable Pay
- Commissions: Paid month following close
- Bonuses: As specified in offer letter
- Referral bonuses: Next pay cycle after hire

### Deductions

#### Pre-Tax
- 401(k) contributions
- Health insurance premiums
- HSA/FSA contributions
- Commuter benefits

#### Post-Tax
- Federal income tax
- State income tax
- Local taxes
- Social Security (6.2%)
- Medicare (1.45%)

#### Voluntary
- Additional 401(k)
- ESPP contributions
- Charitable giving

## Payroll Changes

### Salary Changes
- Effective date: Usually 1st of month
- Requires HR and Finance approval
- Signed documentation required
- Equity adjustments separate

### Tax Withholding
- Update W-4 via HRIS
- Changes effective next pay cycle
- State forms may be required
- Consult tax advisor for advice

### Direct Deposit
- Update via HRIS self-service
- Requires voided check for verification
- Takes 1-2 pay cycles to activate
- Can split between 2 accounts

## Time Tracking

### Exempt Employees
- Track PTO only
- No overtime tracking
- Flexible work hours

### Non-Exempt Employees
- Daily time entry required
- Overtime >40 hours/week
- Meal breaks tracked
- Manager approval required

### Overtime Policy
- Time and a half for >40 hours
- Requires pre-approval
- Comp time not permitted
- Recorded in HRIS

## Payroll Taxes

### Employer Taxes
- Social Security (6.2%)
- Medicare (1.45%)
- Federal Unemployment (FUTA)
- State Unemployment (SUTA)
- Workers' Compensation

### Filing Schedule
- 941: Quarterly federal return
- 940: Annual FUTA return
- State returns: Per state requirements
- W-2: By January 31

## Year-End Process

### December
- Verify employee information
- Confirm benefit elections
- Bonus processing
- Last expense reports

### January
- W-2 distribution (by Jan 31)
- 1099s for contractors
- Year-end reconciliation
- ACA reporting (if applicable)

### Corrections
- W-2c for errors
- Amended returns if needed
- Employee notification
- Penalty considerations

## Payroll Questions

### Common Issues
- Missing hours: Contact manager immediately
- Wrong deductions: Submit ticket to HR
- Bank issues: Verify account information
- Tax questions: Consult tax professional

### Contacts
- Payroll questions: payroll@company.com
- Time tracking: HRIS help portal
- Tax forms: HR department
- Direct deposit: Finance team

## Compliance

### Labor Law Compliance
- Minimum wage verification
- Overtime calculations
- Final pay timing
- Record retention (4 years)

### Privacy
- Payroll data confidential
- Access limited to Finance/HR
- Secure transmission only
- No email with SSNs
