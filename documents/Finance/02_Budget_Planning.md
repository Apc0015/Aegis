# Budget Planning Process

## Annual Budget Cycle

### September: Planning Kickoff
- Strategic planning alignment
- Department budget templates distributed
- Timeline and expectations communicated
- Prior year actuals review

### October: Department Budgets
- Department heads submit draft budgets
- Line item justification required
- Headcount planning submitted
- Capital expenditure requests

### November: Consolidation and Review
- Finance consolidates all budgets
- Executive team review sessions
- Variance analysis vs strategic plan
- Iteration and negotiation

### December: Final Approval
- Board of directors review
- Final adjustments
- Budget locked
- Communication to company

- Software licenses (annual): $200K

## Quarterly Re-Forecasting

### Q1 Review (March)
- Actuals vs budget analysis
- Q2-Q4 adjustments
- Headcount tracking
- Major variance explanations

### Q2 Review (June)
- First half performance
- Full year re-forecast
- Strategic initiative updates
- Hiring plan revisions

### Q3 Review (September)
- Year-end projections
- 2025 budget prep begins
- Capital planning for next year
- Scenario planning

## Spending Guidelines

### Approval Limits
- Department head: $5,000
- VP: $10,000
- CFO: $25,000
- Board: >$25,000

### Recurring Expenses
- New subscriptions require Finance approval
- Annual contracts >$10K need competitive quotes
- Auto-renewal notifications sent 30 days prior

### One-Time Expenses
- Must have budget line item
- Business justification required
- 3 quotes for >$5,000
- ROI calculation for >$10,000

## Variance Reporting

### Monthly
- Actual vs budget by department
- Variance >10% or $5K explained
- Trends and anomalies highlighted
- Corrective actions proposed

### Quarterly
- Deep dive with department heads
- Forecast revision discussion
- Strategic alignment review
- Board reporting preparation
