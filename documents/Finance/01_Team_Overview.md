# Finance Team Overview

## Mission
Ensure financial health, compliance, and strategic resource allocation to support company growth.

## Team Structure (4 members)

### Leadership
- Controller (1)

### Finance Operations
- Senior Financial Analyst (1)
- Financial Analyst (1)

### Accounting
- Senior Accountant (1)

## Functional Areas

### Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A)
- Budgeting and forecasting
- Variance analysis
- Board reporting
- Strategic planning support

### Accounting
- Month-end close
- Accounts payable/receivable
- Payroll processing
- Financial statements

### Compliance
- Tax preparation and filing
- Audit coordination
- Regulatory reporting
- Internal controls

### Treasury
- Cash flow management
- Banking relationships
- Investment management
- Credit facilities

## Key Responsibilities

### Monthly Close
- Complete by 10th business day
- Journal entries and reconciliations
- Financial statement preparation
- Variance analysis

### Budget Process
- Annual budget development (Q4)
- Quarterly re-forecasting
- Department budget reviews
- Capital expenditure tracking

### Board Reporting
- Monthly board deck (10 slides)
- Financial metrics dashboard
- Cash runway projection
- KPI tracking

## Key Metrics
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR)
- Burn rate and runway
- Gross margin
- Operating expenses
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)

## Tools
- NetSuite (ERP)
- Adaptive Insights (planning)
- Bill.com (AP)
- Expensify (expense management)
- Stripe (payments)
