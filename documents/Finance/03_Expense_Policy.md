# Expense Policy

## General Principles
- Spend company money like your own
- Get value for every dollar
- Be reasonable and prudent
- When in doubt, ask Finance

## Eligible Expenses

### Travel
**Flights**
- Economy class for flights < 6 hours
- Business class allowed for >6 hours
- Book 14+ days in advance for best rates
- Use company travel portal

**Hotels**
- Up to $250/night in major cities
- Up to $175/night elsewhere
- Conference hotels may exceed limit with approval

**Ground Transportation**
- Taxis, rideshare, public transit: OK
- Car rental: Requires manager approval
- Personal vehicle mileage: $0.65/mile

**Meals**
- Breakfast: $20
- Lunch: $30
- Dinner: $60
- Alcohol: Reasonable amounts with meals only

### Business Expenses
**Meals with Others**
- Customer meetings: No limit (be reasonable)
- Team meals: $75/person
- Team events: Pre-approved budget

**Software and Tools**
- Under $100/month: Department approval
- $100-500/month: Manager approval
- Over $500/month: Finance approval

**Office Supplies**
- $50/month discretionary per person
- Bulk orders through Operations

**Professional Development**
- Books: $100/month
- Courses: $2,500/year budget
- Conferences: Pre-approved

### Non-Reimbursable
- Personal expenses
- Gym memberships (use wellness stipend)
- Commuting costs (use commuter benefits)
- Clothing and accessories
- Traffic/parking tickets
- Mini-bar charges
- Spouse/family travel (unless approved)

## Expense Submission

### Requirements
- Submit within 30 days of expense
- Original receipts required
- Business purpose documented
- Attendees listed for meals

### Process
1. Pay with personal card or corporate card
2. Capture receipt photo
3. Submit via Expensify within 30 days
4. Manager approval required
5. Reimbursement via payroll (2 weeks)

### Corporate Cards
- Available for frequent travelers
- Monthly statements reviewed by Finance
- Personal charges must be repaid immediately
- Card can be revoked for violations

## Approval Levels

| Expense Type | Amount | Approver |
|--------------|--------|----------|
| Any expense | <$100 | Manager |
| Travel | $100-500 | Manager |
| Travel | $500-2,000 | Director |
| Travel | >$2,000 | VP |
| Software | $100-500/month | Director |
| Software | >$500/month | Finance |
| Events | Any | Finance + VP |

## Audit and Compliance

### Spot Checks
- Random expense review quarterly
- Pattern analysis for anomalies
- Policy violation follow-up

### Consequences
- First violation: Written warning
- Second violation: Required pre-approval for 6 months
- Third violation: Disciplinary action
- Fraud: Termination and legal action

## Questions?
Contact finance@company.com for clarification on any expense.
