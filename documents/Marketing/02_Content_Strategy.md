# Content Marketing Strategy

## Content Pillars

### Educational Content
- Industry trends and insights
- Best practices guides
- Thought leadership
- Research reports

### Product Content
- Feature announcements
- Use case documentation
- Customer stories
- Product comparisons

### Community Content
- User spotlights
- Community events
- Behind the scenes
- Company culture

## Content Types

### Long-form
- Whitepapers (quarterly)
- E-books (bi-monthly)
- Research reports (2 per year)
- Blog posts (weekly)

### Short-form
- Social posts (daily)
- Email newsletters (weekly)
- Videos (monthly)
- Infographics (monthly)

### Interactive
- Webinars (monthly)
- Tools and calculators
- Assessments
- Quizzes

## Distribution Channels

### Owned
- Company blog
- Email lists
- Social media
- Website

### Earned
- Guest posts
- PR mentions
- Industry publications
- Partner channels

### Paid
- Sponsored content
- Social ads
- Native advertising
- Retargeting

## Content Governance

### Approval Workflow
1. Writer submits draft
2. Editor review
3. SME review (if technical)
4. Legal review (if needed)
5. Final approval
6. Publish

### Brand Guidelines
- Tone: Professional but approachable
- Voice: Expert, helpful, confident
- Style: AP Stylebook
- Visual: Brand style guide
