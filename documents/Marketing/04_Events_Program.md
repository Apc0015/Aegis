# Events and Webinars Program

## Event Strategy

### Tier 1: Major Conferences (2 per year)
- Industry flagship events
- Booth sponsorship
- Speaking opportunities
- Budget: $50K per event

### Tier 2: Regional Events (4 per year)
- City-specific meetups
- Partner co-hosted events
- Customer dinners
- Budget: $10K per event

### Tier 3: Virtual Webinars (Monthly)
- Educational content
- Product demos
- Customer panels
- Budget: $2K per webinar

### Tier 4: Owned Events (1 per year)
- User conference
- Company summit
- Training workshops
- Budget: $100K

## Event Planning Timeline

### 6 Months Before
- Venue selection
- Contract negotiation
- Save the date campaign

### 3 Months Before
- Registration opens
- Speaker confirmations
- Marketing campaign launch

### 1 Month Before
- Final headcount
- Materials production
- Staff assignments

### 1 Week Before
- Logistics check
- Tech rehearsal
- Travel coordination

### Day Of
- Setup and registration
- Execution
- Content capture

### Post-Event
- Lead upload and follow-up
- ROI analysis
- Thank you communications

## Virtual Event Production

### Technology Stack
- Zoom Webinars
- Hopin (for larger events)
- StreamYard (for production)
- Slido (for engagement)

### Format Options
- Single speaker presentation
- Panel discussion
- Fireside chat
- Live demo
- AMA (Ask Me Anything)
- Workshop

### Engagement Tactics
- Polls during session
- Q&A at end
- Resource downloads
- Social sharing
- On-demand recording
