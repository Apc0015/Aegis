# Demand Generation Playbook

## Lead Generation Channels

### Paid Search (Google Ads)
- Budget: $15K/month
- Target: Product-category keywords
- Metrics: CPL < $200, Quality Score > 7

### Paid Social (LinkedIn)
- Budget: $10K/month
- Target: Job titles, company size
- Metrics: CPM < $50, CTR > 0.4%

### Content Syndication
- Budget: $5K/month
- Partners: NetLine, DemandWorks
- Metrics: Cost per lead $150-250

### Events
- Budget: $8K/month
- Mix: Virtual and in-person
- Metrics: Cost per lead $300-500

## Lead Scoring Model

### Demographic Score
| Attribute | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Target industry | +10 |
| Company size 500+ | +15 |
| Decision maker title | +20 |
| Work email | +5 |

### Behavioral Score
| Activity | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Website visit | +1 |
| Pricing page view | +10 |
| Content download | +5 |
| Demo request | +25 |
| Email open | +1 |
| Email click | +3 |

### Thresholds
- MQL: 50 points
- SQL: 75 points
- Hot lead: 100 points

## Nurturing Campaigns

### Welcome Series (New subscribers)
1. Welcome email (Day 0)
2. Product overview (Day 3)
3. Customer story (Day 7)
4. Demo invitation (Day 14)

### Re-engagement (Cold leads)
1. "We miss you" email
2. New feature announcement
3. Final attempt with offer
4. Remove from list

## Lead Routing

### Automatic Assignment
- Enterprise (>1000 employees) → Senior AE
- Mid-market (100-1000) → AE
- SMB (<100) → Self-serve

### SLA
- MQL to first touch: 5 minutes
- SQL to meeting: 24 hours
- Hot lead: Immediate call

## Tools Stack
- HubSpot (marketing automation)
- Google Ads
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager
- ZoomInfo (data)
- Mutiny (website personalization)
