Build brand awareness, generate demand, and drive customer engagement.

## Team Structure (5 members)

### Leadership
- CMO (1)
- Marketing Director (1)

### Specialists
- Senior Marketing Manager (1)
- Marketing Manager (1)
- Marketing Specialist (1)

### Brand and Communications
- Brand strategy
- Public relations
- Executive communications
- Awards and recognition

### Demand Generation
- Paid advertising
- Content marketing
- Email campaigns
- Event marketing

### Product Marketing
- Positioning and messaging
- Competitive intelligence
- Sales enablement
- Launch management

### Digital Marketing
- SEO/SEM
- Social media
- Website management
- Analytics

## Key Metrics

### Brand
- Share of voice
- Brand awareness surveys
- Media mentions

### Demand Gen
- Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)
- Cost per lead
- Lead-to-opportunity conversion
- Pipeline influence

### Engagement
- Website traffic
- Email open/click rates
- Social media followers
- Content downloads

- Digital advertising: 40%
- Events: 25%
- Content: 20%
- Tools and tech: 10%
- Brand: 5%
